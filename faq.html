Questions? We Have Answers

Everything you need to know before you book with K. Brooks.

Free QuoteCall (703) 401-4207

Do I need to prepare the items?

No. Just point — we do ALL the lifting and loading. You do not need to move anything to the curb or sort it beforehand.

How is the price calculated?

Price is based on volume — how much of our truck your items fill. We always confirm the price on-site before we start.

Do you offer same-day service?

Yes, in most cases. Call before noon and we will do our best to get you on the schedule.

What do you do with the junk?

We donate usable items to local Northern Virginia charities, recycle whenever possible, and responsibly dispose of the rest.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. K. Brooks is fully licensed and insured for residential and commercial junk removal across Virginia.
